“This advanced system uses computer vision and sensors to detect early signs of fish mortality using a Raspberry Pi and camera module. It tracks fish behavior—like fin inactivity and side-floating—using YOLOv8 and optical flow analysis. The system also detects rainfall events and immediately notifies farmers, helping them prepare for sudden environmental changes. Real-time alerts with image evidence are sent via mobile or web, enabling quick action and reducing fish loss in aquaculture.
