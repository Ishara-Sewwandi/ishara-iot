🎨 WHAT THE RESULTS SHOW:

  • Bounding boxes around detected fish
  • Health status labels (Healthy, Bacterial, Fungal, etc.)
  • Confidence scores (percentage)
  • Color-coded boxes:
    🟢 Green  = Healthy
    🟠 Orange = Bacterial infection
    🟣 Purple = Fungal infection
    🔵 Blue   = Parasitic / White Tail
    🔴 Red    = Dead

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

💡 TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS:

  ✓ Use clear, well-lit images
  ✓ Resolution > 640x480 recommended
  ✓ Fish should be clearly visible
  ✓ Avoid heavily cluttered backgrounds
  ✓ JPG, PNG, or BMP formats
